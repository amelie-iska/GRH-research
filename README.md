# GRH research

A public research workspace for auditing and developing surface-order and Hilbert–Pólya approaches to the generalized Riemann hypothesis, with synchronized concise and extended manuscripts and exact Lean4 checks.

**Current result:** a corrected conditional spectral criterion, convergent arithmetic approximants with explicit errors, an explicit arithmetic Abel trace-class family, and rigorous obstructions to several supplied proof steps. **A complete proof of GRH has not been established.** In particular, the claimed prime/geodesic identification is false, and the absolute prime-tail bound used in the separate GRH PDF diverges in the asserted range. These require mathematical replacements. See [audit](planning/AUDIT.md) and [open obligations](planning/OPEN_OBLIGATIONS.md).

The current [physical-radicand sieve application](research/physical-sieve-and-coherent-main.md)
uses de Faveri’s pinned arXiv:2610.04045v1 Theorem1.1 on the actual sixth-power-free
row family, preserving every raw zero and the arbitrary overlapping sixth factor.
For1<=H<=D its unsigned envelope is O(B²(HD)^epsilon D H^(1/6)):
critical power **9/8**, improving previous7/6 by1/24.
The scoped original unweighted A6=1 signed slice has power **13/12**.
The fixed-profile signed target3/4, principal/short target assembly and independent
arithmetic determinant/zero-location bridge remain open. This is an application of
an explicit external preprint theorem, not a newly proved sieve or a GRH/Lean result.

The previous [ordinary-conductor comparison](research/mixed-conductor-and-learning.md)
bounds the original full-mask remainder by `H + D H^(1/6) + (DH)^(2/3)`,
independently of the cutoff. At `H=D^(3/4)` its historical leading power was7/6,
leaving a factor `D^(5/12)` above the target. The signed covariance and positive
arithmetic determinant/zero-location bridge remain open. A separate executed
finite learner certifies every correlation and both signed-observable errors.
These are independently reviewed prose and exact finite results; no new
analytic/category Lean or GRH proof is claimed.

The new [moving-profile construction](research/profile-uniformity-and-coherent-means.md)
uses the original coefficients and nonprincipal sixth-power rows. A common
profile W_D that varies with scale gives a lower bound of order D H^(1/6);
this is not a counterexample for fixed W. An exact original joint example
also rules out deleting the orientation mean by variance-only centering.

## Projective multiplicity and a derived branch repair

The [next order-interface result](research/theta-projective-multiplicity.md) now treats **arbitrary** order-linear endomorphisms of finite projectives. Their ordinary determinants specialize to sixth powers; bounded perfect-complex ratios retain the obstruction even after cancellation, excluding both a single local quadratic and the full curve zeta. Generically the ordinary ratios are cubes. This strengthens the earlier identity-tensor test, without excluding alternative determinant categories or semilinear actions.

A constructive repair is available: the actual hereditary-branch column lattice defines a derived functor Perf(Λ)→Perf(Q[[u]]) taking a basic projective to rank one. Its determinant on the earlier external arithmetic matrix is the local factor once, without taking a root. This repairs multiplicity, **not the independent arithmetic action**. The selected branch determinant fails full branch-swap compatibility; strict central-fixed rotation on a rank-one branch lattice is also impossible. Ramification plus a changed lattice gives a precisely qualified rotation model. Full arithmetic descent and the global positive spectral comparison remain open.
