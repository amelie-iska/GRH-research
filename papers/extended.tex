\title{Auditing Surface-Order Approaches to GRH: Detailed Proofs and Research Interfaces}
\author{GRH Research Project\\\small Working manuscript; authorship to be confirmed}
\begin{document}\maketitle\begin{center}\status\end{center}
\begin{abstract}
We audit proposed surface-order and Hilbert--P\'olya arguments for GRH.
We retain a conditional spectral-approximation criterion and prove three
obstructions: integral hyperbolic matrices have no integer prime-power norms,
integral cyclic induction fails already for the Klein four group, and
absolute prime tails diverge throughout the proposed critical-strip range.
We also exhibit determinant information lost by symmetrization and a direct
failure of the supplied diagonal diagnostic. These results identify concrete
replacements needed for a complete arithmetic spectral construction.
They do not establish GRH. A small Lean 4 companion verifies selected exact
arithmetic obstructions; the analytic arguments remain prose proofs.
\end{abstract}
