\begin{abstract}Adaptive outward dyadic arithmetic and theta-integral quadrature give a requested-error construction for three fixed completed Dirichlet functions. Fresh samples and executed rational readout training certify the supplied uniform $10^{-24}$ examples on the stated compact; no practical extreme-precision runtime bound is asserted. Separately, a fixed-twist sextic Hecke-family moment improves an original masked remainder estimate, while retaining a positive cutoff loss. We give the precise external analytic inputs and distinguish these results from zero-location and spectral determinant claims. No proof of GRH or complete Lean formalization is asserted.\end{abstract}
