What is being replayed?

What remains a proposal?
Full upstream language-model training, optional memory/GFlowNet/persistence heads, non-Gaussian photonic networks, physical hardware and NVIDIA GPU runs are not reproduced here. Finite carriers forget parts of the infinite dessin order. The arithmetic determinant bridge remains open.
