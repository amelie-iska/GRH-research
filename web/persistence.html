What is exact here?
Frozen symmetric integer graph labels define a precise inclusion diagram. Boundary matrices, cycle spaces and induced ranks are computed over F₂ with integer bit arithmetic.
The labels come from floating-point trace-distance estimates rounded upward to bins of 1/1000. Those estimates are not interval certificates. Input population births are fixed and reused at the output; training epochs and unrelated channels are not filtration axes.
