Electron density clouds: motion, nodes and interference.
These are normalized isolated-hydrogen wavefunctions in atomic units. The 1s–2p superposition produces genuine quantum beats; the complex 2p orbital has phase winding +1 around its nodal axis. These are different phenomena from an SSH winding or a material Chern number. Density gained/lost is zero for a stationary eigenstate; choose the 1s–2p superposition to see quantum-beat redistribution.

What keeps the partitions separate?
Whole parent, conformer, gauge, intervention and task descendants stay together. Chemical and physical similarity edges are closed transitively before component-level stratified allocation. The resulting threshold-relative audit is not a guarantee against all possible leakage or overfitting.
