A width limit with specified assumptions
Independent one-qubit features φ(x)=√2⟨Z⟩=√2 cos(w·x+b), Gaussian w and uniform phase b give K(x,y)=exp(−‖x−y‖²/2). Only the readout is trained in this experiment. This is a different limit from enlarging the four-qubit generative networks.
Max-plus arithmetic gives a piecewise-linear operation. A tropical variety or HMS comparison additionally needs a valued algebra, geometric data and a categorical construction. Over a valued field, leading coefficients can cancel: val((1+T)+(−1+T))=1 although both summands have valuation zero. In characteristic two the sum is zero. Finite-field algebraic coefficients do not supply ordered probabilities or ordinary gradient calculus.

Free-group covers and actual dessin modules
F₂ is a free group; 𝔽₂ is a coefficient field. The theta graph has fundamental group F₂ and a degree-three tree cover. Its contracted Cayley graph is degree four. The genus-two/four selectors instead give the degree-five/nine cover trees of their contracted dessins. Clean white bivalent vertices are suppressed in these tree drawings. Filling the dessin faces adds surface relations: the universal cover of a compact genus-two surface is not this tree.
Module calculations use the genuine infinite genus-two polynomial dessin algebra. B is its one-arrow string at vertices 0 and 4; it has Ext¹(B,B)=1 and an explicit nonsplit self-extension. The graph-only deck-Hom example is separately labelled and is not substituted for this algebra.
