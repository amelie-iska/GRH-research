What is being replayed?

What remains a proposal?
Full upstream language-model training and its optional heads, non-Gaussian photonic networks, physical hardware and NVIDIA GPU runs are not reproduced here. The executed finite Quantum TokenGT collection includes the saved concept critic and continuous GFlowNet; local persistence examples retain their own evidence.
