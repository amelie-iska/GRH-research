What the identity guarantees

The antisymmetrizer is an orthogonal projector in characteristic zero. Its trace equals its rank, N(N−1)/2. The Fierz identity follows from an orthonormal basis of all complex matrices with the scalar identity removed. These finite representation identities supply color factors; a Yang–Mills continuum measure and mass gap require the separate analytic arguments described in the paper.

Soundness, termination, and what is formalized

Every cancellation uses U†U = I. Composition in a typed context preserves equality, and each cancellation decreases the gate count by two. The simplifier terminates, although it does not find every possible equality. A learned policy may choose among certified rules without weakening their soundness.

The Lean 4 module checks a typed sequential linear-map syntax, its contexts, and finite rewrite derivations. The paper proves tensor-context and measurement consequences. This browser uses double-precision arithmetic; its residual is a finite numerical check. Qubit 0 is the most-significant basis bit. The explicit limit is six qubits and 80 gates.

Why this is a counterexample to a categorical computational prohibition

Under exact integer arithmetic the same fixed recurrence recognizes the unbounded one-bracket Dyck language. The proof is by induction on the count and persistent error flag. A finite automaton cannot recognize that language. The growing count uses O(log L) bits on length L; fixed bounded hardware still has finite resources.

That proves neural representability. It does not show that gradient descent discovers this recurrence, nor that a particular language model generalizes to arbitrary depth. The manuscript separates those questions and reviews the primary linguistic experiments and their published critiques.

05 · Mechanism and information

The same observations.
Different counterfactuals.

Both models produce X = Y with equal probabilities for 0 and 1. Their responses to an intervention differ. The finite Boolean mechanisms have exact ReLU implementations.

Mechanism A

X = U; Y = X

Mechanism B

X = U; Y = U

With U unobserved and fair, do(X = 0) gives Y = 0 surely in A, but P(Y = 1) = ½ in B. No observational-only learner can distinguish these identical input distributions. Additional causal assumptions or interventions are required.

The argument in the paper

A direct critique with explicit obligations.

The chapter rejects Chomsky’s categorical claim that neural methods cannot yield understanding in principle. It proves concrete capabilities, distinguishes idealized expressivity from learned behavior, and includes Hunter’s critique of the “impossible languages” experiment. An operational semantic specification can be tested; a universal claim about consciousness is not inferred from it.
