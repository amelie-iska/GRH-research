What is checked—and what is not

The page recomputes the clean-dessin permutation identities, transitivity, Euler characteristic, binary stabilizer ranks, and CSS commutation. A separate worker runs the existing dense 18-qubit Float64 kernel, checks normalization and compares the measured expectation with an independent analytic identity. New inputs invalidate old results; delayed responses cannot overwrite current state.

The physical model is a constructed toric-code spin Hamiltonian H = −Σ star-X − Σ face-Z. Its 36 dessin darts describe incidences; they are not 36 independent qubits. The flat view repeats boundary representatives of one periodic surface. No Belyi map, continuum embedding theorem, observed signal, quantum advantage or hardware realization is inferred.
