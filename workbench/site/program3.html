01 / EMITTED ARITHMETIC

Does the endomorphism actually pay?

Counts come from the emitted X/CX/CCX stream, including signed decomposition and uncomputation. Logical all-to-all gates, not error-corrected physical resources. This bounded compiler is not a large-curve efficiency claim.

Encoding, invalid inputs and clean registers

Affine x/y words have a unique infinity flag. Invalid point words are fixed by the in-place translations. The browser executes every primitive gate on the chosen basis input, compares an independent affine group calculation and checks all scratch/decomposition bits return to zero. Full-domain and coherent GPU checks are separate recorded evidence.

02 / LIFT, COARSE-GRAIN, COMPARE

Covering degree is not neural width.

The cyclic gentle base has all length-two compositions forbidden. A sheet shift lifts its arrows and relations. The corresponding integral order additionally requires zero total voltage around uv = πe; the zero special fiber alone does not enforce that.

Six learned rotation parameters define a trace-preserving 4 → 2 → 1 channel. The discarded qubits are traced out, not postselected. The browser replays the trained gates on [a, a/2, −a, a/3]; this input is separate from the recorded Ising Gibbs-state fit.

An explicit infinite-feature reference

Independent single-qubit RY feature circuits converge to a declared Gaussian radial-basis kernel. The horizontal axis is the number of independent circuits. It is neither covering degree nor an unrestricted theorem for deep, entangled, jointly trained quantum networks.

03 / REAL MAPS, FROZEN SEARCH

What survives the full search?

The displayed tail is a conditional phase-surrogate rank over all 72 locations/scales and three methods. It is not the probability that CCC is true. WMAP products share the sky and observations; they are sensitivity comparisons, not independent discoveries.

Original observed results remain fixed. Changing the injection runs a labeled counterfactual with unchanged learned weights and calibration; it does not revise the observation.

The final shared-phase family validation flagged 9 of 256 independent null skies (3.52%) at the nominal 5% threshold; its conditional Wilson interval is 1.86%–6.55%. This finite null check does not establish invariance of real foregrounds or instrument noise under the phase group. The large change under a common ILC reference remains an essential sensitivity result.

Resolution, masks, calibration and source provenance

The three maps are NASA WMAP DR5 ILC and foreground-reduced V/W temperature products. Native Nside512 nested maps in millikelvin are converted to microkelvin, averaged to Nside16, band-limited at ℓ = 32, and additionally smoothed by 5°. KQ85 and a latitude cut define the fixed mask. This broad-scale test does not reproduce degree-scale Hawking-point studies.

Nulls randomize harmonic phases while preserving their magnitudes. Instrumental anisotropy, foreground-cleaning uncertainty and the harmonic reconstruction are not fully modeled. Followup frequency-difference/common-reference tests are explicitly exploratory and do not replace the original scan.
