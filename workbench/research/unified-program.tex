\section{The organizing principle}

This program is organized around a concrete mathematical demand: a proposed connection must carry its objects, its maps and the identities those maps preserve. A dessin is not used as an ornament for a neural network. A quiver is not used as a synonym for any directed picture. An order is not interchangeable with every integral algebra. Each construction earns its place by doing calculable work.

The common substrate is a typed finite presentation together with its realizations. A cellular dessin supplies permutations, incidence and a surface. Quivers with relations supply module categories and complexes. Integral lifts retain arithmetic that disappears over a field. Metrics turn selected complexes into Hodge operators and energy forms. Binary reduction produces commuting Pauli checks when the relevant chain identity survives. Graph-to-graph models propose maps and refinements; an independent checker decides which algebraic, numerical or probabilistic statements those proposals satisfy.

The ambition is substantial: use this substrate to organize spectral arithmetic, nonlinear dynamics, quantum information, operator learning and structured inference within one inspectable program. The standard is equally substantial. Every claimed bridge must specify its realization map; every limiting claim must control the limit; every empirical gain must survive a comparison that charges its information and computational costs. A skeptical label is not a refutation. The appropriate response to a construction is to examine its hypotheses, follow its proof and reproduce its tests.

\section{From a relation to topology, energy and quantum checks}

A representation of the gentle bound quiver
\[
 2\xrightarrow{a}1\xrightarrow{b}0,\qquad ba=0,
\]
is precisely a two-step complex $C_2\xrightarrow{d_2}C_1\xrightarrow{d_1}C_0$. A closed oriented cellular surface supplies such a complex over $\mathbb Z$ through its boundary maps. The incidence matrices and the relation $d_1d_2=0$ are explicit finite data. Choosing positive inner products gives the finite Hodge operator
\[
 \Delta_1=d_2d_2^*+d_1^*d_1.
\]
For every $u$, its quadratic form is $\|d_2^*u\|^2+\|d_1u\|^2$. Consequently its kernel is exactly $\ker d_2^*\cap\ker d_1$. The positive metric is part of this realization; a general change of module basis is not automatically unitary.

Reducing an integral cellular complex modulo two gives $H_XH_Z^T=0$ for the corresponding incidence choices. Pauli operators $X(a)$ and $Z(b)$ commute exactly when their binary supports have even intersection. Thus the chain identity becomes a quantum stabilizer commutation identity. Over a connected closed genus-$g$ surface, the familiar rank calculation gives $2g$ encoded qubits when the surface and rank hypotheses hold. The existing surface-code implementation checks these hypotheses on its actual cellulations and executes their preparation circuits.

This passage is a specific realization, not a claim that every gentle representation is a quantum code. Conversely, a finite binary check does not recover all integral torsion. The BGG and orders chapters retain that arithmetic layer rather than discarding it for computational convenience.

\section{An explicit order whose special fiber is gentle}

There is a direct bridge between an integral order and a gentle relation, not merely a resemblance between their diagrams. Let $R$ be an integral domain, $K$ its fraction field, and $0\ne\pi\in R$. Define
\[
 \Lambda_\pi=\left\{\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\\pi c&d\end{pmatrix}:a,b,c,d\in R\right\}
 \subset M_2(K).
\]
It is a unital subalgebra, free of rank four over $R$, and spans $M_2(K)$. For a Dedekind domain it is an order in the sense fixed in the original monograph. Localization at a prime generated by $\pi$ yields the corresponding local tiled-order example.

\begin{proposition}[Multiplication, generic fiber and gentle specialization]
In coordinates $(a,b,c,d)$ the multiplication is
\[
 (a,b,c,d)(e,f,g,h)
 =(ae+\pi bg,\;af+bh,\;ce+dg,\;\pi cf+dh).
\]
Let $e_1,e_2$ be the diagonal idempotents, $u=E_{12}$ and $v=\pi E_{21}$. Then
\[
 uv=\pi e_1,\quad vu=\pi e_2,\quad u^2=v^2=0.
\]
If $R/(\pi)=k$ is a field, the special fiber $\Lambda_\pi/\pi\Lambda_\pi$ is the four-dimensional radical-square-zero two-cycle algebra over $k$, which is gentle. Its generic fiber is $M_2(K)$. The determinant of the trace pairing in the basis $(e_1,u,v,e_2)$ is $-\pi^2$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Ordinary matrix multiplication gives the coordinate formula, closure and associativity. The identity has coordinates $(1,0,0,1)$. The four basis matrices are $R$-linearly independent and span $\Lambda_\pi$; after extending scalars, $E_{21}=\pi^{-1}v$, so all four matrix units are present. The displayed arrow relations follow by direct multiplication.

Modulo $\pi\Lambda_\pi$, all four coordinate classes remain independent. In particular $v$ does not vanish merely because its displayed matrix entry is divisible by $\pi$: the lower-left entry of $\pi\Lambda_\pi$ is divisible by $\pi^2$. Both length-two cycles vanish in the quotient. The two idempotents and two arrows give exactly the radical-square-zero two-cycle presentation. Each arrow has one forbidden successor/predecessor and no allowed length-two continuation, proving the gentle conditions and finite dimensionality. The trace-pairing matrix has diagonal entries one on the idempotent coordinates and an off-diagonal $\pi$ block on $(u,v)$, so its determinant is $-\pi^2$.
\end{proof}

This example identifies precisely where arithmetic enters. The generic fiber, special fiber and integral lattice answer different questions. The relation $uv=0$ in the special fiber is the shadow of $uv=\pi e_1$ in the order. A model trained only on the special-fiber graph cannot reconstruct the deformation parameter unless it is supplied or inferred from additional data. The implementation checks the coordinate realization, multiplication and specializations; selected identities are formalized in Lean over a general commutative ring.

\section{A finite spectral gap, proved and executed}

On the checked $3\times3$ torus, take nine star checks $A_v$ and nine plaquette checks $B_f$, acting on eighteen edge qubits. For positive $J_X,J_Z$, set
\[
 H=-J_X\sum_v A_v-J_Z\sum_f B_f.
\]
The commuting checks have two independent global relations, $\prod_vA_v=I$ and $\prod_fB_f=I$, and sixteen independent generators. The ground space therefore has dimension four.

\begin{theorem}[Exact finite toric-code spectrum]
For this cellulation, the energy labeled by even integers $a,b\in\{0,2,4,6,8\}$ is
\[
 E_{a,b}=-J_X(9-2a)-J_Z(9-2b),
\]
with multiplicity $4\binom9a\binom9b$, combining equal energies when necessary. The ground energy is $-9J_X-9J_Z$ and the gap is
\[
 \Delta=4\min(J_X,J_Z).
\]
For a normalized state with ground-space probability $p_0$,
\[
 \langle H\rangle-E_0\ge\Delta(1-p_0).
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Commutation permits simultaneous diagonalization. The two global relations force an even number of negative star eigenvalues and an even number of negative plaquette eigenvalues. Independent stabilizer generators realize every compatible character with the same four-dimensional sector multiplicity: the character projector has trace $2^{18}/2^{16}=4$, since nonidentity Pauli products have zero trace. Counting the choices of violated checks gives the multiplicity formula, whose sum is $4\cdot2^8\cdot2^8=2^{18}$.

Every nonzero excitation has at least two violations of one type, so the energy increment is at least $4\min(J_X,J_Z)$. A single-edge Pauli $Z$ flips its two distinct endpoint stars, and a single-edge Pauli $X$ flips its two distinct neighboring plaquettes; the bound is attained. Finally, decompose a normalized state into the simultaneous eigenspaces. Each nonground component costs at least $\Delta$, giving the expectation inequality.
\end{proof}

The program executes this Hamiltonian's preparation, controlled rotations, CNOT circuit and readouts. The new explorer links the selected physical edge to its dessin cycles, attention coordinates and quantum gates. Independent NumPy, browser and cuStateVec implementations agree at recorded tolerances. This is a completed finite spin-system result. A four-dimensional continuum Yang--Mills construction requires the additional analytic transition below; using the same phrase ``mass gap'' does not identify the two operators.

\section{The continuum transition as a precise theorem}

The finite-to-continuum step can be stated without handwaving. Let $A_n\ge0$ be self-adjoint operators on a common Hilbert space, and let $P_n$ be their kernel projections. Suppose $A_n$ converges to a nonnegative self-adjoint $A$ in strong resolvent sense, $P_n\to P$ strongly for an orthogonal projection $P$, and every $A_n$ satisfies a common gap $\Delta>0$ off its kernel.

\begin{proposition}[Gap transport with a controlled vacuum projection]
Under these assumptions, $P$ is the kernel projection of $A$, and
\[
 \operatorname{spec}(A)\subseteq\{0\}\cup[\Delta,\infty).
\]
To retain a nonzero vacuum, require $P\ne0$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Write $R_n=(A_n+I)^{-1}$ and $c=(1+\Delta)^{-1}$. Spectral calculus gives
\[
 R_nP_n=P_n,\qquad 0\le R_n\le P_n+c(I-P_n).
\]
Strong resolvent convergence implies $R_n\to R=(A+I)^{-1}$ strongly. Because $\|R_n\|\le1$, passing to strong limits in the first identity yields $RP=P$. Passing to quadratic forms in the second gives $R\le P+c(I-P)$. Thus $P$ lies in the eigenspace of $R$ at one, and hence in $\ker A$. On its orthogonal complement $R\le cI$; therefore no additional vector can belong to $\ker A$. Spectral calculus on that complement yields $A\ge\Delta I$. These two statements prove the conclusion.
\end{proof}

For Yang--Mills, a successful application must construct the continuum theory, the compatible operator realization, the scaling, the resolvent limit and the nonvanishing vacuum projection. The proposition specifies the exact job of that analytic construction. Numerical gaps without these transports do not enter its premises. This is an actionable theorem architecture: it says what must be proved and what follows once it is proved.

\section{Probability diffusion as an open quantum evolution}

Let $P$ be a finite nonnegative column-stochastic matrix. Define
\[
 K_{ij}=\sqrt{P_{ij}}\,|i\rangle\langle j|,\qquad
 \Phi_P(\rho)=\sum_{i,j}K_{ij}\rho K_{ij}^*.
\]
Then $\sum_{i,j}K_{ij}^*K_{ij}=I$, so this is a completely positive trace-preserving map. Direct multiplication gives
\[
 \Phi_P(\rho)=\operatorname{diag}(P\,\operatorname{diag}\rho).
\]
Thus diagonal probability evolution is exactly the classical Markov update. If $P$ is also row-stochastic, the channel is unital. The periodic heat stencil in the workbench supplies such a $P$ for $0\le r\le1/4$.

This establishes an actual quantum-channel realization of finite diffusive probability transport. It also makes the information cost visible: off-diagonal coherence is removed. The channel is not a unitary relabeling of diffusion, and a velocity field is not automatically a density matrix. Nonlinear fluid and quantum-fluid models require their own state encoding and physical evolution law. The original monograph's finite energy, pressure, projection and residual identities supply exact interfaces for those extensions.

The 8 September 2026 Navier--Stokes release is a separately attributed forced-flow singularity construction with public Lean artifacts. Its force, support, regularity and norm hypotheses remain attached to the theorem. The present program reconstructs and studies its mathematical interfaces; that attribution is not replaced by claiming that a finite heat channel independently reproves the analytic result. \section{Controller-to-circuit robustness}

The new hybrid example composes a classical coordinatewise normalized max-plus map with quantum rotations. For score perturbation at most $\delta_s$ and value perturbation at most $\delta_v$,
\[
 y_c=\max_j(s_j+v_{jc})-\max_j s_j
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 |y'_c-y_c|\le2\delta_s+\delta_v.
\]
This follows from nonexpansiveness of finite maximum. If $\theta_c=b_c+g y_c$, the four rotation angles change by at most $g(2\delta_s+\delta_v)$ each.

For a single rotation, $\|R_Y(\theta)-R_Y(\theta')\|\le|\theta-\theta'|/2$. A telescoping product of unitary gates therefore changes the normalized state by at most half the sum of angle errors. For an observable of operator norm at most one, inserting and subtracting the cross term bounds its expectation error by twice the state error. Hence this four-angle controller satisfies
\[
 |\langle Z\rangle'-\langle Z\rangle|
 \le4g(2\delta_s+\delta_v).
\]
This is a concrete neural-to-quantum error budget. A hard score router needs an additional winner margin because its selected value may jump at a tie. The distinction is visible in the interactive controls and tested independently.

\section{The arithmetic spectral target}

A Hilbert--P\'olya application requires an arithmetic identity, not a visual match between spectra. Here is a precise sufficient interface. Let $T_\chi$ be a compact self-adjoint Hilbert--Schmidt operator with real eigenvalues $\mu_j$. Its regularized determinant is
\[
 D_\chi(z)=\prod_j(1-z\mu_j)e^{z\mu_j}.
\]
The product converges locally uniformly: for sufficiently small $w$, $\log(1-w)+w=O(|w|^2)$, and $\sum_j\mu_j^2<\infty$. Its zeros are exactly the nonzero reciprocal eigenvalues, with multiplicity; away from them the convergent logarithmic tail is nonvanishing.

If an independently constructed entire completion of the relevant $L$-function satisfies
\[
 \Xi_\chi(s)=e^{g_\chi(s)}z^{m_\chi}D_\chi(z),
 \qquad z=-i(s-1/2),
\]
with entire $g_\chi$ and integer $m_\chi\ge0$, every zero has real $z$, and therefore $\Re s=1/2$. The proof is the product's zero characterization and self-adjointness. The arithmetic task is the construction of $T_\chi$ and the exact identity, without assuming the desired zero locations in the construction. Orders, characters, trace formulas and module transports can organize that task; the identity remains the criterion that makes the spectral route decisive.

\section{One program, distinct routes to evidence}

The resulting architecture is coherent. Dessins and cellular maps supply finite presentations. Gentle and BGG structures organize relations and resolutions. Orders track integral specialization. Hodge operators and stochastic channels furnish analytic and probabilistic realizations. Surface spin systems furnish quantum realizations. ToricGT, TropicalGT and Mora-3 supply graph-valued proposal and approximation mechanisms. Exact witnesses, Lean declarations, numerical residuals and held-out code lengths determine which transitions have been justified.

For language and cosmological inference, compressibility establishes predictive structure relative to a code, not intentionality by definition. The new control study uses genuine human speech with independently established provenance and genuine detector strain with a known physical event. It charges its model and side information and includes negative results. The purpose is to make a proposed information-bearing grammar confront observations under a protocol capable of disproving it.

The exposition therefore makes no appeal to modesty and no appeal to prestige. A claim is as strong as its hypotheses and proof. A failed boundary case is repaired, not hidden. A positive result is stated directly, not buried under ritual disclaimers. The reader is invited to inspect the complete chain from presentation to realization to prediction. That is the foundation on which a broad mathematical program can be built and extended.
