\section{What a discovery test must discriminate}

Predictable physical structure, an effective grammar, a semantic code and intentional communication are different hypotheses. A periodic or low-dimensional dynamical process can be highly compressible without a sender. A grammar can be a useful description without being a natural language. A code is supported by a stable relation between symbols and independently accessible outcomes; intentional communication additionally requires evidence about an encoder, intervention or communicative context. Model expressivity does not identify those hypotheses from indistinguishable observations.

A realistic staged test begins with held-out prediction against strong physical and statistical baselines. It then asks whether a structured grammar improves compression after charging its parameters and side information, whether its rules transfer to new systems or conditions, and whether interventions discriminate the proposed semantics from alternatives. Known human communication supplies a positive control; physical noise, periodic dynamics and measurement artifacts supply distinct controls. A failure on a positive control limits the decoder; success on a physical series does not establish intent.

\section{Coding definitions with an honest denominator}

For a lossless declared byte representation $x$ and a normalized sequential distribution, ideal bits per byte is $-\log_2p(x)/|x|_{\rm bytes}$. A finite precision implementation may additionally need probability quantization, coder termination and a decoder convention. The study reports an ideal likelihood length rather than claiming to have emitted that arithmetic-coded bitstream.

For a directed graph $G$ with attributes $X$, a useful two-part accounting is
\[
 L(G,X)=L(\theta)+L(\text{representation})+L(G\mid\theta)
       +L(X\mid G,\theta).
\]
Divide by a fixed canonical original byte count, or separately report bits per vertex, edge and attribute. Adding graph tokens to the denominator without charging their information can artificially improve a score. Direction, order, types, weights and missingness belong in the graph code unless recoverable from public conditions. A graph inferred using hidden target values is not free side information.

The empirical task uses fixed-point integers at each source's published precision. Eight-bin quantization is fitted on training data only. The symbol likelihood is supplemented by an exact signed integer residual: with $d=v-r_b$, encode $z=2d$ for $d\ge0$ and $z=-2d-1$ otherwise using Elias gamma on $z+1$. Its length is $2\lfloor\log_2(z+1)\rfloor+1$. This permits exact reconstruction of the represented integer given the bin representative; it does not preserve the source CSV formatting or every original instrument measurement. First differences are reconstructed from explicitly supplied lag-context seeds. The two-part result charges model values, metadata, seeds and any retrieval bank. The interpreter and universal code convention are shared fixed conditions, not asserted to have zero absolute description length.

A computable compressor supplies a description-length upper bound on Kolmogorov complexity up to the decoder/program constant. It does not compute Kolmogorov complexity, whose general exact computation is unavailable to an effective finite procedure. Compressor comparisons, prequential coding, minimum-description-length accounting and normalized compression distances are operational proxies with biases. Representation choice, sample size and compressor overhead can dominate them. Compression alone is not a test for an intentional message.

\section{Executed bounded experiments}

Four data sources were actually downloaded: NOAA monthly Mauna Loa background CO$_2$, SILSO monthly sunspot number, NOAA Ni\~no-3.4 anomaly, and PhysioNet MIT-BIH record 100 MLII. The first three are observational summaries; the sea-surface index includes a reconstruction model. The ECG experiment uses the first 24,000 samples from one recording. No clinical conclusion or patient-held-out generalization follows. Source URLs, exact byte hashes, units, version qualifications and failures are retained with the data release.

The native comparison uses the actual pinned Mora-3 TokenGT and tropical modules, instantiated as small one-layer, 16-wide, two-head encoders with a task-specific categorical readout. It is not the full approximately 80M model, a pretrained checkpoint or the ongoing 24+2 campaign. Two seeds and 80 optimizer steps per architecture/dataset give 16 bounded component runs. These are actual training executions, not synthetic demonstrations of an untrained operator.

Inputs contain eight strictly past first differences represented by eight node tokens and seven forward edge tokens. Ordered endpoints and node positions retain direction. Targets, future values and target-derived edges are absent. A chronological 60/20/20 partition with discarded boundary contexts separates training, validation and test. Normalization and quantizer construction use training only. Checkpoint selection, baseline order/smoothing and retrieval-mixture selection use validation only. Two seeds do not establish reliable population-level architecture superiority.

The analogy adapter retrieves training-window targets using normalized embeddings from the trained native model. Its bank is temporally disjoint from validation and test. A fixed directed-chain semantics supplies the relation check; no general semantic equivalence between physical systems is inferred. Its memory cost is included in the two-part metric. Exact source-to-target path witnesses and ground-rule deduction are separately implemented and tested.

Exploratory block bootstrap intervals assess variation in paired per-example codelength differences; their stationarity assumptions are not established for all four series. Circularly shifting labels relative to contexts is reported only as a descriptive diagnostic, not a valid universal permutation $p$-value. No multiple-comparison-adjusted discovery of language or a physical message is asserted. The Gutenberg English control uses actual human-authored text with its provenance establishing communication independently of the score.

\section{Additional realistic tests, not executed results}

\paragraph{CMB/CCC template grammar.} Use native Planck temperature/polarization products, masks, beam transfer and noise/foreground uncertainty. Freeze a source grammar and decoder on independent simulations or include their entire fitting/selection procedure inside null calibration. Hold out sky regions only with a dependence-aware design; neighboring pixels are not independent examples. Compare likelihood and code length with standard cosmological and foreground alternatives. A retained anomaly is evidence against a specified null, not automatically CCC or an intentional cross-aeon message. Source: \url{https://pla.esac.esa.int/}.

\paragraph{Gravitational-wave graphs.} Use GWOSC strain and data-quality flags with separately defined off-source controls. A directed multichannel graph should encode actual sample timing and detector metadata available to the decoder. Compare a learned residual model with established waveform/noise models and charge template selection. Test transfer across held-out events and observing conditions, retaining instrumental transients as alternatives. Neither structured residuals nor interdetector dependence demonstrate language. Source: \url{https://gwosc.org/}.

\paragraph{Physiology.} Extend from the one-record pilot to patient-group-disjoint public physiological records, then test across devices and conditions. A grammar can encode waveform phases or state transitions; counterfactual clinical claims require interventions or justified causal models beyond a recording. De-identification, source licenses and native sampling/units remain mandatory. Source: \url{https://physionet.org/}.

\paragraph{Fluid and transport processes.} For measured flow, preserve sensor geometry and sampling uncertainty. For JHTDB or PDEBench simulations, label solver-generated data explicitly, hold out initial-condition and parameter families, and charge numerical error. Test whether a directed flux grammar improves held-out rollout coding while preserving conservation under independent replay. Simulation data are not experimental observations. Sources: \url{https://turbulence.pha.jhu.edu/} and \url{https://github.com/pdebench/PDEBench}.

\paragraph{Known communication.} Use speech/transcript or documented animal-vocalization/context data with speaker/individual/session-held-out splits and verified redistribution rights. Separate signal decoding from compositional generalization. Match acoustic nuisance factors and evaluate controlled semantic interventions where ethically available. A statistically ordered call sequence is not by itself evidence of human-like syntax. The current English text control is a simpler executed reference, not a substitute for those acoustic experiments.

\paragraph{Information transfer rather than resemblance.} Across any pair of processes, predeclare a unit-consistent relational map, source-only memory bank and admissible target subpaths. Compare transferred proposals with target-only and unconstrained retrieval baselines, test wrong-direction and branch-deletion controls, and require an independent semantic or dynamical residual bound before calling the transfer a deduction. This directly connects analogy training to the graph grammar and BGG relation-checking program without conflating them.
