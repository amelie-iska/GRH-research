\chapter*{Scope}
This supplement extends the GRH-research monograph. It separates constructive mathematics, finite Lean proofs, exact arithmetic tests, numerical diagnostics, actual small native-model experiments and proposed integrations. The empirical study uses measured or observation-derived data and separately reports model and retrieval costs. No hidden physical message, cosmic communication, full-model training completion or quantum advantage is claimed. Repository commits and data receipts are the authority for execution status; mathematical constructions alone are not empirical results.
