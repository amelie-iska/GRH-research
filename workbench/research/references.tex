\chapter{Primary sources and source-pinned implementation register}
\label{ch:wb-sources}
\noindent Reading scope: the native theorem/code passages discussed in the audit were inspected at the commits below. External entries are attributed results, not claims that their complete proofs were reverified in Lean. The present review checked primary metadata and the relevant accessible statements; selected sections of Yun, ConvexTok, the syntax--semantics paper and the BGG papers were examined. Unread surrounding arguments are not represented as a complete full-text review. Numerical experiments have a separate source/hash/run record. No third-party full text is redistributed.
