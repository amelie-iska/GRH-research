Which loops disappear when a face is filled?
HoTT’s hubs-and-spokes construction attaches a disk with a fresh hub and a continuous circle-indexed family of paths. The finite drawn rays below sample a two-dimensional region. A graph star alone is not the disk; deleting its vertex is not a punctured disk.
The punctured genus-two dessin surface has free group F₄. Capping its face adds [a₁,b₁][a₂,b₂]=1 and changes the covering problem. Graph covers are trees; filled-surface covers include lifted cells. Homology alone misses this: H₁ is Z⁴ in both cases.
