Where the guarantee stops
The validated theta/Mellin labels are frozen at 64 nodes per conductor. The program scans degrees 0–63 and accepts a requested rational tolerance only if its exact budget has strict slack. Otherwise it refuses. The smallest computed base budgets are about 7.29 × 10⁻¹⁷, 1.74 × 10⁻¹⁶ and 3.37 × 10⁻¹⁶ for conductors 3, 4 and 5; these are limits of this certificate, not impossibility results for approximation.
New sample truncations and transcendental precision are still needed for arbitrary requested accuracy. This is a finite linear readout with a documented objective, not a trained quantum architecture or an arithmetic spectral determinant. It supplies neither zero-location control nor a proof of GRH. No new Lean theorem is claimed.
