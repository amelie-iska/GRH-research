# Bessenrodt–Holm: full reading and reuse

Read 2026-11-03: Christine Bessenrodt and Thorsten Holm, *Weighted locally gentle quivers and Cartan matrices*, arXiv:math/0511610v1 (24 November2005), all24pages, all sections and displayed proofs, including the finite-dimensional reduction, critical case, duality and polygon/Harer–Zagier computation. This is a full textual review, not independent verification of all referenced classification results. Theorem1 is usable with finite quiver, quadratic monomial locally gentle relations and formal commuting arrow weights. Its numerator runs over primitive full-relation cycles, with factors 1-(-1)^length times cycle weight; denominator runs over primitive permitted cycles with factors1-cycleweight. Weights live in an augmentation-complete power-series ring. Specializing all weights to1 through denominator zeros is not justified. Cycles are up to rotation, not all repetitions. Finite-dimensional and infinite-dimensional cases are distinguished; a formal rational expression does not prove an arithmetic analytic continuation.

Section3.3 defines the grading shift (M<n>)_d=M_(d-n), while Proposition3.4 displays negative shifts in the linear resolution. Under the stated shift convention the generators need positive degree n. The new paper fixes this explicitly. Proposition3.4 supplies the forbidden-chain resolution, with exactness proof left to the reader; our new article gives the basiswise proof, augmentation and all maps. The graded algebra with arrow degree1 is Koszul. Arbitrary weighted positive degrees need not be standard Koszul.

Complementing quadratic relations exchanges permitted and forbidden paths. The weighted Cartan inversion substitutes negative arrow weights once. In an already signed Ext series no further substitution is appropriate. Indexing must be declared before attaching any transpose. This corrects the original project preprint, not the substance of the Bessenrodt–Holm formula.

All determinant proof cases in sections4.1–4.6 were read. Their weighted cycle formula survives our independent derivation: for source/target incidence matrices S,T, diagonal arrow weight D, and permitted/forbidden transition matrices U,V, U+V=TSD and C=1+SD(1-U)^(-1)T. Sylvester's identity gives det(C)det(1-U)=det(1+V). Locally gentle transition graphs are disjoint chains/cycles, giving exactly the stated factors. An independent reviewer checked dimensions, ordering, one-cycle signs, augmentation completion and the critical case. This compact proof avoids invoking the original row-reduction induction; no claim of novelty is made.

The critical two-vertex four-arrow example has a permitted four-cycle and forbidden four-cycle with the same commuting weight product. Their determinants cancel, detC=1, although infinite resolutions remain. Thus a scalar Cartan determinant loses substantial module/derived information. The formula computes a formal invariant. It does not supply prime lengths, Frobenius, a determinant realization of a completed number-field L-function, positivity or a Hilbert–Pólya proof.
