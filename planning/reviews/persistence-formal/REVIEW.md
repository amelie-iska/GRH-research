coefficients. Thus regularity is proved for these x,y and is not a hidden
assumption of the claimed concrete resolution. Free-term projectivity is

R-module quotient by that ideal, as required.

Hom-composition statements quantify over all R-linear maps to this residue
module. These are the correct induced Hom differentials. They establish the
vanishing mechanism after forgetting grading, rather than a falsely inferred
exactness after specialization.

There is no hidden GRH/positivity/zero-location premise or circular assumption.
The abstract regular-pair lemma's hypotheses are appropriate and explicitly
discharged at its application. No project-specific axiom is present.
