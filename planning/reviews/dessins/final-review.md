## Local details corrections communicated to the author

References are to the details file as initially reviewed; wording may have been corrected concurrently.

1. **Line 4 — exclude vacua from the unique-branch statement.** “Every permitted path has exactly one such expression on exactly one branch” must say **every positive-length permitted path**. A vertex idempotent has two diagonal branch occurrences, exactly as the next sentence explains. The order theorem itself treats this correctly; this is a local contradiction in the expanded proof, not a failure of the order construction.

2. **Line 31 — replace the incorrect boundary explanation in the Ext-product proof.** The sentence “Where the prescribed prefix ends, the next differential is zero precisely for the forbidden join” is not a valid projective-level statement. In the prefix-discard comparison map, that boundary is generally multiplication by the next arrow and is nonzero; both sides of the chain-map identity agree. The needed argument is: discarding the fixed prefix on precisely those forbidden-word summands that contain it defines comparison maps commuting with the differentials; the concatenated indexing word exists exactly when the join is forbidden; evaluation of the two basis classes on it gives 1. If the join is permitted, no such indexing word occurs and the product is zero. This preserves the correct complementary-relation/Koszul-dual result and gives an actual comparison-map justification. The normal right-module/Yoneda-opposite convention must still be stated, as it is in the draft.

3. **Lines 192 and 213 — define and rename the coefficient pairing.** J(P_Nv) and J(v) appear without an explicit symbolic definition, while J already denotes the arrow ideal. Define, for example, ℐ(v)(z)=Σv_nz_n and replace these occurrences by ℐ. The pairing is complex linear, not the conjugate-linear Riesz map; its formulas and norm estimates are otherwise correct.

4. **Optional precision at line 56.** Replace the ambiguous phrase “Its trace on M^⊗3” by the literal projected-trace equality `Tr((Y/3)M^⊗3)=χ_(2,1)(M)`. The trace of Y/3 alone is just its rank; the stated character computation is the trace of its product with M^⊗3. The shipped checker computes the correct product, so this is a wording clarification rather than a failed calculation.

## Main mathematical checks

**Schur and LR constructions.** The positive-degree Young functors preserve monomial zero relations, while the empty partition is correctly excluded as a module-valued unit. The inherited tensor-power Hilbert norm gives the needed Schur norm bound even for nonnormal matrices. The three-cycle invariant ring has generators of arrow degrees 3,6,…,3d; its equal multidegree pieces have the claimed Schur basis. Cauchy summation under rR₀²<1 gives an actual Hermitian PSD kernel without requiring the matrices themselves to be Hermitian. The natural multiplication section and wedge intertwiner give the explicit two-dimensional LR splitting, including singular arrow maps. The failures of termwise exactness and of the fixed central R-action are genuine, correctly interpreted counterexamples.

**Fock C*-extension.** The compact ideal proof uses exact vacuum matrix units and keeps terminal sectors separate. The quotient CK family has nonzero vertex projections and the inherited continuous gauge action. These satisfy the actual gauge-invariant uniqueness theorem of Bates–Pask–Raeburn–Szymański, Theorem 2.1, printed p.311; no exit condition is being omitted. The lift `W=L_cQ_a+(1−Q_a)` is an isometry, whereas the tempting unrestricted lift is not. Its finite defect consists of the first |c| paths starting at that state and counts precisely the incident darts in their terminal sectors. The negative unsigned incidence map, Smith form and displayed K-groups follow, including the Z/2 in the nonbipartite case. This is a Fock compact extension, not an automatic identification with a graph boundary quotient or the completed order.

**Number operator.** The maximal domain, finite-support core, self-adjointness and compact resolvent hold. Creators and adjoints preserve that domain and have the stated bounded commutators. Positive-length multiplicities are 2E, giving the p>1 summability threshold and scalar traces. The e^(−tN) and e^(−tN²) formulas are correctly distinguished. The character trace is correctly restricted to Re s>1, and membership of χ(N) in the smaller Fock C*-algebra is not asserted. The ungraded status and trivial ordinary odd Fredholm class are explicitly stated.

**Abel and Galerkin estimates.** The dyadic block majorants, closed Q_J formula and u-tail B₀/√J are correct. With J≥1, the cutoff contains the first coordinate, so the rank-one determinant subtraction cancels its 1 exactly. The raw endpoint has the correct sign and produces S_N rather than the Abel-corrected sum T_N. The Galerkin estimate accounts separately for truncating the arithmetic range vector and the coefficient functional. Isometric insertion preserves all nonzero singular values and hence the trace norms and determinants. The completed functional scales the coefficient pairing but retains the unscaled first-coordinate subtraction, giving gL and gS_N exactly.

**Metric and arithmetic scope.** For the stated inner-product convention the fixed-range symmetry formula is correct. The two rounded exponential subsequences give opposite nonzero limits of the vertical coefficient ratio, excluding one common positive metric for this actual family. The argument does not exclude other realizations or altered-domain constructions indiscriminately. The elliptic coordinate scaling is a projective isomorphism of the complex Belyi pairs, while the exact good-reduction counts at 7 distinguish their H¹ Frobenius polynomials. Descent data are therefore necessary for recovery from complex/combinatorial data alone; there is no asserted obstruction to a theory including such data. The weight-one elliptic center and the Dirichlet center are correctly separated.

**Conditional final criterion.** For the initial primitive nonprincipal Dirichlet scope, the completed target is entire and nonzero. Locally uniform entire convergence of nowhere-zero scalar factors times self-adjoint characteristic polynomials forces every target zero to be real by Hurwitz on disks off the real axis. The manuscript explicitly leaves that construction and convergence unproved. The Connes interval form, core, parity, normalization and ground-state Fourier comparison remain actual hypotheses; neither the Fock vacuum nor the Cauchy kernel discharges them. Deligne purity is used as the finite-field model, not as an imported number-field purity theorem.

**Other explicit category calculations.** The nodal normalization proof uses BM inside the generic module, avoiding a nonflat tensor-product identification. The lattice AR sequences and central reduction diagram are correct. In the Chebyshev quotient, the open killed-arrow ideal, endpoint-local-freeness distinction, intrinsic diamond AR factorization scope and failure of ambient extension closure are retained. The square-zero quotient has the correct relative KG/CB dimension 2, not a claimed ambient rank; it contains no nonzero R-free lattice.
