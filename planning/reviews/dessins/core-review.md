## Specific corrections

1. **Line 120: require a meaningful kernel radius.** The theorem writes `||M_i||≤R_0` and `0≤r<R_0^{-2}` without requiring R_0>0. The allowed input all M_i=0 can have R_0=0, for which the displayed reciprocal radius is undefined in ordinary real notation. Replace the condition by `r≥0 and rR_0²<1`, or explicitly require `R_0>0`. The product formula, norm majorant and PSD argument are otherwise correct. The former condition also naturally includes all zero matrices.

2. **Line 102: specify the characteristic-polynomial sign.** With f equal to multiplication by u−λ, the Hom determinant is det(u_N−λI). Under the usual monic convention χ_(u_N)(λ)=det(λI−u_N), it equals (−1)^d χ_(u_N)(λ). Replace “its determinant on N is the characteristic polynomial” by that equality, or use multiplication by λ−u instead. This does not affect the unit/nonextension argument or the zero locus, but exact determinant identities should keep this sign.

3. **Line 242: make the real-zero theorem's hypothesis precise.** The reference to “a core” is correct as a warning but imprecise if the paragraph will serve as a sufficient interface. Say that the real distribution's specified form is lower bounded and essentially self-adjoint on the trigonometric-polynomial core, with a simple isolated lowest eigenvalue and interval-reflection-even eigenfunction. This is the precise statement of Connes–van Suijlekom, [2511.23257v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.23257v1), Theorem 6.1, p.13. The length-parity vacuum does not establish these data. The present paragraph already correctly denies such an automatic application.

The known `WeilI`/`WeilII` citation-key mismatch at line 244 was reported by the author before this review and is not a new finding. The bibliography uses `DeligneWeilI`/`DeligneWeilII`.

## Checks supporting the main conclusions

**Order and resolutions, lines 4–75.** The successor conventions are consistent: τ=σ⁻¹ is permitted, φ=ασ⁻¹ is forbidden, and complementary relations give σ#=φ⁻¹ and αφ=τ. The branch-order description includes the necessary equal constants and distinguishes the central parameter from path degree. The L+1 bound ensures that removing a cycle leaves a nonempty suffix, so multiplication by the central sum restores precisely the chosen branch. The forbidden-word resolution proof excludes cancellation by separate surviving path supports and extends coefficientwise to the completion. Euler–Cartan inversion uses the sign only once. The arrow-state proof has TS=U+V and correctly gives det C=det(I+V)/det(I−U). No arithmetic Euler product is inferred from this identity.

**Semi-invariants, lines 78–102.** With the stated point-evaluation convention, the weight b_i−a_i is correct. Schofield–Van den Bergh's determinant-spanning statement is not restricted to acyclic quivers; characteristic zero and the lifting of fixed weights on stable closed loci are retained. The argument Ext¹(M,N)↪coker Hom(f,N) remains valid when f is not injective. Thus, on the square finite locus, Hom(M,N)=0 indeed forces Ext¹(M,N)=0 even for a noninjective f. This is not being confused with an equality of that cokernel or with higher Ext vanishing. The explicit square-quiver example has Ext² and Ext³ equal to k: its Hom cochain maps repeat a, d, b, c, with only a nonzero. The continuous finite-dimensional nilpotence and scaling-to-zero argument correctly concerns the reduced variety.

**Schur section, lines 105–135.** The unnormalized Young operator has the correct hook scalar and extracts one Schur copy. Positive degree is essential for preservation of zero maps and is stated. Naturality of LR decompositions gives simultaneous arrow intertwiners for the specified synchronous vertexwise tensor operation, with no claim that this is ⊗_A or an exact functor. The three-cycle invariant ring, degree 3j generators, equal multidegrees and Schur basis are valid. Arbitrary X_3 on the section X_1=X_2=I verifies independence. The kernel uses overline(M_j), not an unjustified transpose/adjoint substitution: ordinary Cauchy characters give exactly this determinant. Restriction of M^⊗m to the Young image gives the claimed norm bound with its induced Hilbert norm; absolute convergence then proves PSD. The empty scalar term and the degeneration of all positive-degree characters on continuous finite-length modules are properly separated.

For a completely unambiguous tableaux convention, details may specify that the LR row-reading word is read right-to-left along successive rows from top to bottom. This is a convention clarification, not a discovered failure in the theorem.

**Fock quotient and K-theory, lines 166–200.** The original graph CK1 collapse needs neither CK2 nor an illicit division: forbidden-successor surjectivity kills every arrow, and incoming-arrow coverage then kills the vertices. The constrained initial projection at line 173 is different and avoids that argument. Vacuum matrix units show the compact ideal is exactly ⊕_v K(H_v), with no cross-terminal compacts. In its quotient the Q_a and L_a are the CK family of the disjoint permitted-state cycles. Their nonzero quotient projections and the inherited continuous length-gauge action meet the exact hypotheses of [Bates–Pask–Raeburn–Szymański, Theorem 2.1, printed p.311](https://nyjm.albany.edu/j/2000/6-14.pdf). No exit condition is needed for this gauge-invariant theorem; using the different Cuntz–Krieger uniqueness theorem would not suffice for cycles without exits.

The specific lift W=L_cQ_a+(1−Q_a) is an isometry: on paths beginning with a it shifts degree by |c|. Its defect is the first |c| such paths. Their terminal vertices enumerate the darts of c, including two appearances when that graph edge is a loop. This gives precisely the stated negative unsigned incidence column. The Smith forms and six-term sequence give the two K-group rows, including the Z/2 in the connected nonbipartite case. No unproved parity sign or omitted terminal sector was found.

**Degree spectrum, lines 202–214.** There are 2E positive-degree basis vectors in every degree and E vacua. The maximal diagonal domain, bounded commutators, compact resolvent and exact p>1 summability follow. The e^(−tN), rather than e^(−tN²), trace is the formula actually stated. The draft explicitly retains the ungraded status, trivial ordinary odd Fredholm class, and lack of zero-ordinate spectral interpretation.

**Abel insertion and metric obstruction, lines 217–232.** The normalized two-path sums form an isometry in one terminal sector. U*U=I proves the exact determinant, and preservation of nonzero singular values proves all trace-norm identities and convergence estimates. The no-conjugation coefficient pairing is correct.
