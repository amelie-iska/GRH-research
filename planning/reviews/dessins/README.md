# Dessin-focused extension and independent review

This revision uses only infinite-dimensional closed locally gentle algebras and completed orders of explicit cellular clean dessins as primary examples. Finite algebras appear only as specified order quotients; categories are not silently identified.

- [Leavitt/Fock and global Belyi inputs](leavitt-review.md): primary definitions and exact extension/index proof.
- [Semi-invariants and nodal lattices](semiinvariant-ar-review.md): weights, homological scope, conductor classification and actual AR factorization.
- [HLS diamond resource](diamond-review.md): arXiv:2110.02777v2, relevant proofs pp.1–13 and16, rendered grids; root-system pp.17–21 not audited.
- [Ringel/domestic resources](added-resources-review.md): arXiv:1407.7470v2 and1506.07703v2 throughout; Ringel §§1–9 and relevant later constructions, not every later quilt proof.
- [Schur/Procesi/LR](schur-invariants-review.md): actual modules, cyclic invariant ring and local Cauchy positivity, plus exactness/central-action failures. The original Procesi and ABW PDFs were inaccessible; precise modern primary accounts and coverage are identified rather than a claimed full reading.
- [Recent NCG](ncg-literature-review.md) and [pinned source artifacts](ncg-source-manifest.json): exact versions, hashes and theorem/page-level coverage through2026.
- [Arithmetic insertion/descent/spectrum](spectral-review.md): independent verification and explicit failure of an intrinsic arithmetic comparison.

The prior four requested-source full-reading reviews remain in ../projective/.
