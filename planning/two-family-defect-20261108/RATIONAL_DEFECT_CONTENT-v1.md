For a good ideal \(a\), its rational content is the largest integer \(m\ge1\) with \((m)\mid a\). Write \(x=(m_x)x^\circ,\ I=(m_I)I^\circ\) and use integer dyads \(M_x\le m_x<2M_x,\ M_I\le m_I<2M_I\). On a split rational prime the rational exponent is the minimum of the two ideal valuations. On an inert prime it is the entire ideal valuation. The fixed ramified primes are excluded by \(S\). Thus \(N(m_x)=m_x^2,\ N(m_I)=m_I^2\). The division is unique, and the two residual physical lengths are \(O(B/M_x^2),O(K/M_I^2)\), where \(B,K\) retain their original meanings as the physical lengths before division. A nonempty residual dyad has length bounded below by a positive constant. The residual \(x^\circ\) is good squarefree, and \(I^\circ\) is good sixth-free. A residual factor of \(I\) may share a prime with its own rational content, with every original power still present.

At fixed contents the exact condition \((x,I)=1\) is the conjunction of
\[
 ((m_x),(m_I))=1,\qquad (x^\circ,(m_I))=1,\qquad
 (I^\circ,(m_x))=1,\qquad (x^\circ,I^\circ)=1 .
\]
The first condition is fixed, the next two are one-array restrictions, and the last uses the same squarefree ideal Möbius expansion as in TWO_FAMILY_CROSS-v1. Fixed rational symbols, phases and raw zeros multiply the two-array sum. All original content and sixth-free predicates are one-array predicates after the contents are frozen. The exact equality projector uses \(Nz_* m_x^2 Nx^\circ=Nk_* m_I^2 NI^\circ\). The log Fourier extension of the actual partner profile uses the physical ratio \(m_I^2 NI^\circ/(m_x^2 Nx^\circ Q_u)\). Hence the exact complement, profile and primal decay still refer to \(B,K\), including at norm collisions.

Their product of square roots has a factor \(1/(M_xM_I)\). Summing over the \(O(M_xM_I)\) integer pairs cancels this factor, and the \(s\)-sum remains harmonic.
