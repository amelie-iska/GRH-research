## Sparse mass after reduction to sixth-free columns

The raw grouped product has squared coefficient mass \(O((DH)^\epsilon PT)\), rather than the full physical length \(P^2T\). To retain this bound through the sieve, write the actual product uniquely as \(\mathfrak s\,b_0 v^6\) with \(\mathfrak s\) supported on \(S\) and \(b_0\) good sixth-free. At fixed \(\mathfrak s,v\), split the squarefree \(h_0\) as \(h_{\rm in}h_{\rm out}\) by the support of \(v\). Then \(h_{\rm in}\mid v\), \(h_{\rm out}\perp v\), and \(h_{\rm out}^2\mid b_0\). \[
 O\!\left(\frac{P^2T}
 {N\mathfrak s\,(Nv)^6\,(Nh_{\rm out})^2}\right)
\]
residual ideals. On nonempty support that quotient is at least a fixed positive constant, so the same bound includes the unit. Counting \(h_{\rm out}\) with \(Nh_{\rm out}\asymp P/Nh_{\rm in}\) gives squared mass
\[
 O\!\left((DH)^\epsilon
 \frac{PT\,Nh_{\rm in}}{N\mathfrak s\,(Nv)^6}\right).
\]
Apply the physical sixth-order moment to \(b_0\) with its signed bounded coefficient, and take the row triangle over the fixed factors. The raw factor \(\psi_u(\mathfrak s v^6)\) is retained as a contraction. The weights sum absolutely since

and the fixed \(S\)-part has inverse-square-root weight. Thus the product has normalized second row moment at most a subpower times
