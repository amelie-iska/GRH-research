## Additional next steps actually implemented and executed

**Uniform reversible field arithmetic.** Carry/borrow, schoolbook multiplication,
constant-divisor binary long division and Fermat inversion are emitted uniformly
as X/CX/CCX gates without enumerating input truth tables. Validity flags, arbitrary
XOR output registers and complete scratch uncomputation are explicit. Fourteen
modulus/operation studies cover primes 7, 13, 31, 251 and 65521, with inversion
bounded through eight bits. Small input domains are exhaustive; wider cases are
seeded samples plus boundary words. The unit suite additionally exhausts complete
input/output domains for selected small cases. This closes the lookup-synthesis
gap for field primitives, not the separate full large-curve elliptic/fault-tolerant
compilation task. Polynomial saved workspace is not claimed low-ancilla optimal.

**Trained cover-compatible neural operator.** A time-conditioned network learns
a convex degree-24 lazy-walk polynomial. Three seeds train on graph sizes 3 and 6
with separate validation times; 63 held-out size/time/seed cases use sizes 9, 15
and 24. Nonnegativity, unit mass, covering intertwining and the corresponding
CPTP random-unitary lift are checked independently of fit quality. The largest
covering residual is 4.440892098500626e-16. The largest learned spectral error is
0.033904781569900844; the analytic Poisson baseline is better, with maximum error
5.214105284520982e-7. Both results are retained. The browser evaluates the exported
network and walk, and its output agrees with Python. No quantum advantage or
continuum interacting-field solution is inferred.

**Higher-resolution CCC control.** The trained QNN, WMAP observations and 72
location/scale templates are retained. An explicitly exploratory precision study
refreshes independent normalization with 512 draws, calibrates the joint maximum
with 4095 shared-phase skies, and validates on 1024 independent draws. The phase
action preserves inter-product harmonic cross-products. The observed rank is
155/4096 = 0.037841796875, with 154 calibration exceedances. Its conditional
exceedance-probability interval is [0.03198996071073251, 0.04389506006676903].
Null validation flags 57/1024 (5.56640625%), interval
[0.04242855159855284, 0.07152105123598544]. These quantify simulation uncertainty
under the stated null, not physical model error or the posterior probability of
CCC. Earlier 511-draw results, common-reference sensitivity and frequency-residual
checks remain separately identified rather than retrospectively overwritten.

**Formal interfaces.** Four new Lean declarations prove power intertwining,
a scalar model-error envelope, the long-division step invariant and the precisely
typed Boolean XOR-output involution. The audit inspects printed types and axioms;
no inference is made from a theorem name alone.
