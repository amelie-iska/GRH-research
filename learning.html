01 · Weighted tokenization

A prediction proposes.
A certificate proves.

Vertices mark positions in a string. Each edge is a permitted token with an integer cost. The model predicts remaining costs and proposes a path; an independent dynamic program checks the optimum.

For every edge, cost = base cost + u × slope. Changing u recomputes the circuit inputs, model output, and exact path certificate. The finite vocabulary is held fixed.

02 · The token feature map

Eight amplitudes.
Six Weyl features.

Each graph token becomes six data angles. Two layers of RY and RZ rotations, followed by controlled-NOT gates, prepare a three-qubit state. The real and imaginary parts of ⟨U⟩, ⟨V⟩ and ⟨UV⟩ become model features.

Final features and exact shift derivative

The derivative applies to each observable feature. The nonlinear model loss requires the chain rule; shifting the whole loss is generally incorrect.

U|j⟩ = exp(2πij/8)|j⟩ and V|j⟩ = |j−1 mod 8⟩. Their real and imaginary expectations are expectations of (W+W†)/2 and (W−W†)/(2i). Statevector expectations here are exact up to floating-point error; physical measurements would require shots.

03 · The rest of the trained model

Attention, incidence,
and learned experts.

Node and edge tokens share an encoder. Their endpoint incidences enter attention scores. A learned mixture combines softmax attention with a max-plus context; four experts each provide two routing slots.

Softmax attention

Expert combination for the selected token

Combination weights sum to one across slots for each output token. Dispatch weights instead normalize across tokens for each slot; they are different matrices. Attention weights describe an internal calculation and do not by themselves establish a causal explanation.

How this connects to the larger project

Weyl clock and shift operators represent a finite noncommutative torus. Toric and semigroup geometry describe separate algebraic constructions in the manuscript. Token-DAG optimization supplies an exact, small benchmark. The surface-code companion contains the actual toric and higher-genus quantum circuits.

The paper specifies how a typed graph model could propose tensor rewrites or cosmological detection statistics. Those applications need their own data, physical models and evaluations; this token benchmark does not establish them.
