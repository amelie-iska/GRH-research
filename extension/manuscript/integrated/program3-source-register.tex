\begin{description}[leftmargin=2.6em,labelwidth=2em]
\item[E1] Roetteler, Naehrig, Svore and Lauter. \emph{Quantum resource estimates for computing elliptic curve discrete logarithms}. ASIACRYPT 2017. \url{https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.06752}. This is a scalable circuit-resource reference, not a performance claim for the bounded compiler implemented here.
\item[Q1] Cong, Choi and Lukin. \emph{Quantum Convolutional Neural Networks}. Nature Physics 15, 1273--1278 (2019). \url{https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.03787}. The present unitary/trace hierarchy is an explicit finite channel inspired by hierarchical coarse graining; it is not represented as a verbatim implementation of every construction in that paper.
\item[Q2] Girardi and De Palma. \emph{Trained quantum neural networks are Gaussian processes}. Version 2, 2026. \url{https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.08726v2}. The light-cone, initialization, kernel and scaling assumptions must accompany any transfer of its trained-network limit.
\item[G1] Assem, Br\"ustle, Charbonneau-Jodoin and Plamondon. \emph{Gentle algebras arising from surface triangulations}. Algebra and Number Theory 4 (2010), 201--229. \url{https://arxiv.org/abs/0903.3347}. The unpunctured marked-surface hypotheses are retained.
\item[G2] De la Pe\~na and Redondo. \emph{Covering functors without groups}. Journal of Algebra 321 (2009), 3816--3826. DOI 10.1016/j.jalgebra.2009.02.023. The finite voltage construction in this volume is specified directly rather than claiming all covering-functor results.
\item[C1] NASA LAMBDA / WMAP Science Team. \emph{WMAP DR5 ILC map description and downloads}. \url{https://lambda.gsfc.nasa.gov/product/wmap/dr5/ilc_map_info.html}; \url{https://lambda.gsfc.nasa.gov/product/wmap/dr5/ilc_map_get.html}. Product resolution, Galactic coordinates and the approximately ten-degree reliability qualification determine this study's broad scales.
\item[C2] NASA LAMBDA / WMAP Science Team. \emph{WMAP DR5 sky maps and masks}. \url{https://lambda.gsfc.nasa.gov/product/wmap/dr5/skymap_info.html}; \url{https://lambda.gsfc.nasa.gov/product/wmap/dr5/masks_get.html}. Native filenames, units, ordering and exact bytes are recorded in the acquisition manifest.
\item[C3] The original GRH-research chapter \emph{Cyclic cosmology} and its frozen source ledger. \url{https://github.com/amelie-iska/GRH-research/tree/fd57c5251bc40c388cc25aa83e25f8a2cdf995b7/extension/review/cosmology}. Original theoretical claims and published observational comparisons remain separately attributed; this continuation is not a replacement for those source papers.
\end{description}
