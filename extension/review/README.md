## What the inventory does not mean

The raw outbound-link extraction contains 409 distinct URL strings, including
fragments, software links, local development addresses and some punctuation
artifacts. It is not a count of 409 distinct research papers. Neither that
inventory nor a file tree is recorded as a full reading or proof audit.

A full review of every linked research document and a Lean formalization of
every theorem requested remain unfinished. No completion statement in the
manuscript, website or release record should erase that boundary.

## New algebraic checks

The exact companion verifies all 257 feasible nonnegative solutions whose ten
matching-semigroup coordinates are at most two, plus larger constructed
examples; it checks 135 block determinant instances; and it directly counts
19 and 323 elliptic-curve points over F17 and F17². The general statements are
proved in the text rather than inferred from these finite tests.

The source ledger includes original author URLs and frozen revisions where
available. It deliberately does not redistribute the uploaded copyrighted
books, their full OCR text, or downloaded third-party research PDFs.
