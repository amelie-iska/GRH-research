# Book fragment: learned finite carriers with an exact physical teacher

The completed surface order, the dessin cellulation, a finite physical model
on that cellulation, and the auxiliary feature circuit have different objects
and dimensions. Our genus-two clean dessin has five quiver vertices and
completed DVR rank50. Its subdivided physical incidence graph K₂,₅ has seven
spin sites and ten incidence edges. A TokenGT input has these seven node
tokens, ten edge tokens and one masked padding position. Three auxiliary
feature qubits per token are neither these seven physical spins nor a
faithful representation of the infinite completed order.

For controls θ=(J₊,J₋,b), the exact coarse action on five retained white spins is

```
Aθ(s)=log(2cosh(b+J₊ Σⱼsⱼ))
     +log(2cosh(−.6b+J₋ Σⱼ(−1)ʲsⱼ))+Σⱼhⱼsⱼ,
hⱼ=.045cos(1.7j)+.02j/5.
```

This is an actual sum over the two discarded black spins. The constant term
is retained. The teacher supplies all32 coarse logweights, logZ and all five
retained magnetizations. The learned models predict the full action; their
partition and observable errors are computed from that predicted action.
This controlled three-parameter family is not an unrestricted family of all
coarse interactions.

A token's three-qubit state is physically normalized and generated by actual
PennyLane RY/RZ, CNOT and RX gates. Six measurable Z/ZZ expectations become
classical token features. The feature-circuit angles beyond the data encoding
are fixed. We train the Q/K/V matrices and a nonlinear classical head. With
S=QKᵀ/√6 and a separate validity mask, the three contexts are
softmax(S)V, max_j(Sᵢⱼ+Vⱼᵣ), and their half-sum. These are classical attention
operations on measured features, not a coherent quantum attention unitary.
The tropical operation here is the max-plus aggregation used in the pinned
ToricGT source. No tropicalization of a Fukaya category follows from it.

We learn three steering directions B and a critic using an explicitly random
intervention code C uniform over six (direction,sign) values. For a base
embedding z, the induced random change is
Δ=tanh(.3z+.7sign(C)B_direction(C))−tanh(.3z).
The loss combines cross entropy for predicting C from Δ and
.08||BBᵀ−I||², with normalized B rows. The empirical heldout estimate of the variational MI-bound functional
log6−CE is1.78844nats. This measures sampled code recoverability for this
intervention distribution; it is not a certified population-MI bound without
sampling uncertainty control and does not establish semantic disentanglement.
A fixed basis vector itself is not a random variable. A ridge decoder maps
steered learned embeddings to physical controls, and their physical effects
are evaluated by the exact teacher.

The latent forest branches along both signs of each direction, retains a
width-three beam and searches depthfour. Its61 evaluated candidates have
exact physical rewards based on logZ and the five magnetizations. The best
reward improves from−1.60383 to−.70865 for the saved target. The remaining
error is substantial. This is finite branching optimization, without claims
about human thought or general reasoning.

Actual finite persistent homology is computed over F₂ using all vertices,
edges and triangles of the Rips2-skeleton, with simplex birth equal to its
maximum edge length. The12-point learned-embedding cloud has one
positive-length H₁ interval[2.50049,2.97133). Target and transferred physical
observable clouds, scaled as(logZ/3,m₀/.25,…,m₄/.25), both have no positive-
length H₁ intervals and have pairwise-distance RMSE.29095. Empty matching
H₁ barcodes alone do not prove a faithful analogy. These are metric-dependent
finite data diagnostics, not a physical topological-order invariant.

The continuous GFlowNet trains a forward Gaussian MLP on a time-expanded
R² latent chart, with three steps and deterministic termination. The root
measure is δ₀; later state measures are Lebesgue. Backward kernels for t=2,3
are N(((t−1)/t)x_t,((t−1)/(3t))I); the final backward move is the unit point
mass at the root. The integrable reward is

```
R(x)=exp(−||x||²/2 + .35(logZ_K₂,₅(.62+.35tanh x₀,.58+.34tanh x₁,.12)−7)).
```

Its bounded physical factor is confined by the Gaussian. We minimize the
squared log trajectory-balance residual on10,000 fixed continuous off-policy
trajectories for1,800steps. Fresh heldout TB MSE is.002579 and on-policy TB
MSE is.002782. Learned lognormalization1.70832 compares with finite-box
quadrature1.70734. Ten thousand new samples give an approximate histogram
TV.04859 against a20×20 midpoint histogram comparator on[-5,5]²
(cell width.5). The separate normalizer quadrature uses201×201 points at
spacing.05. Both are verification grids; trajectories and policy states are
continuous, and tail/quadrature errors are not certified. This is a two-dimensional physical latent chart, not a
claim that all six pooled coordinates have a learned full-support flow.

Actual quantum error correction is executed separately. A Bell reference
and a three-data-qubit repetition encoder undergo independent BitFlip noise,
parity-syndrome projection, conditional X corrections, decode and partial
trace. The mixed-state SDK density agrees with an independent sum of eight
error patterns. The logical error is3p²−2p³, so entanglement fidelity is
1−3p²+2p³. At p=.1 this is.972 versus.9 unencoded. The code protects against
one X error under ideal corrections, with no phase-error protection or
universal fault tolerance.

The recovered channel is additionally inserted as an actual ideal feature-
memory layer in the saved TokenGT inference pipeline. After preparing each
three-qubit token state, encode each logical qubit in a disjoint repetition
block, apply independent X memory noise, recover and then measure features.
For a Z product of weight w, the feature attenuates by(1−2p_eff)^w, where
p_eff=p,f(p),f(f(p)) at recovery levels0,1,2. The tensor-channel identity
applies even to entangled token feature states. Those levels require3,9,27
physical qubits per token with ideal encoding and recovery.

not a9/27-qubit noisy-gate circuit or fault-tolerant recovery implementation.
There is no phase-error or correlated-noise protection claim.

There is also an instructive exact cellular CSS calculation on the same
actual genus-two dessin. Put qubits on its ten physical edges, X checks at
its seven vertices and Z checks at its single face. The F₂ incidence matrix
has rank6. Each edge appears twice in the single face boundary, so the face
check is zero. Thus k=10−6−0=4=2g, but the code has X-distance1 and Z-distance4:
an exact[[10,4,1]] code with no useful memory protection. Explicit enumeration
checks these ranks and distances. Surface genus and four encoded logical
qubits therefore do not imply good topological error correction. A useful
surface-code proposal needs a refined family of cellulations, bounded local
checks, growing primal and dual systoles, an explicit noise model and a
verified decoder. None is supplied by the token embedding alone.

The mathematical connections delimit research directions rather than close
these gaps. For real actions aᵢ, max aᵢ ≤ τlogΣexp(aᵢ/τ) ≤ max aᵢ+τlogN;
therefore a zero-temperature max-plus limit has a precise finite-error bound.
Replacing finite-temperature decimation by max discards entropy and can
change the constant, so this limit is not our executed RG teacher. To connect
tropical mirror-symmetry geometry one would additionally need specified
valuations, degeneration data and the relevant disk/curve counts; an
attention semiring does not supply them.

Likewise, the [Lekili–Polishchuk surface result](https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.06370v2) concerns homologically smooth
**graded gentle** algebras with nonempty boundary, stops and a line field.
To apply it to any chosen quotient of our completed order, one must specify
that quotient and grading, verify smoothness and the required surface data,
and construct the relevant category/functor. No general equivalence of the
infinite completed order with the three-qubit carrier is asserted. A possible
categorical loss would compare explicitly defined Hom/Ext or intersection
invariants after those hypotheses are established; it is not trained here.

Materials or generative quantum-AI applications would require a stated
Hamiltonian family, physical datasets or a verified quantum oracle, symmetry
and locality constraints, and heldout physical observables or experimental
validation. The present exact-RG teacher is a small reproducible testbed for
such proposals, with no materials-discovery or arithmetic conclusion.
