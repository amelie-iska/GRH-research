Read the exact identity and the geometric boundary

On (ℝ / 2πℤ)², ω = ∂ₓu₂ − ∂ᵧu₁ = 2 sin x sin y e−2νt. The mean energy is E = ½ average |u|² = e−4νt/4, and dE/dt = −νe−4νt. The pressure gradient cancels the nonlinear convective term. The velocity, pressure, divergence, curl and energy are checked independently against symbolic expressions.

The surface display transports coordinate arrows through a torus parameterization. Its visual arrow lengths are not intrinsic speed measurements. This exact two-dimensional flow is not an example of three-dimensional finite-time blowup.

See the order, covering degree, and complex-structure obstruction

Set A = J − 4I. Then A² = 2I and M(a,b) = aI + bA = [[a − b, b], [b, a + b]]. Matrix multiplication gives M(a,b)M(c,d) = M(ac + 2bd, ad + bc), while det M(a,b) = a² − 2b². Thus det J = 14, and the toral covering x ↦ Jᵏx has degree 14ᵏ. The complete fibres use integer quotient representatives, rather than randomly sampled points.

J is not complex-linear for any real linear complex structure: no commuting real matrix I satisfies I² = −1. This arithmetic covering is not automatically an endomorphism of a complex elliptic curve. The displayed identities contribute an explicit order-theoretic interpretation of one auxiliary construction; they do not replace the released proof’s analytic estimates.

The circuit computes s = 1.4⟨Z₀⟩ − 0.8⟨Z₁⟩ + 0.6⟨X₀X₁⟩ and νθ = 0.02 + 0.28/(1 + e−s). The position grid has Δx = 2π/16 and Δt = 0.1, so r < 0.195 < ¼. Each Uδ is a periodic translation, hence unitary. Kraus operators √(1 − 4r)I and √rUδ give complete positivity and trace preservation. The same mixture is unital. For the displayed diagonal states, von Neumann entropy equals the Shannon entropy computed from the probabilities.

Each parameter-shift feature derivative is [f(θ + πeⱼ/2) − f(θ − πeⱼ/2)]/2. The diffusivity derivative then uses the sigmoid chain rule. The code does not apply parameter shift directly to the nonlinear sigmoid. Independent dense-matrix references check all gates, expectations, norm and derivatives.

The displayed position calculation exactly follows the diagonal sector of this quantum channel; it does not evolve arbitrary coherences. The independent Python checks also exercise a coherent mixed state and Kraus/Choi identities on a separate 3 × 3 position grid. Why the scaling calculation is insufficient as a blowup proof

The formal radial and axial scales are τ1/2 and τ1/2−h, velocity is τ−1/2−h, and volume is τ3/2−h. Their energy product has exponent 1/2 − 3h, positive throughout the displayed interval. This accounts for compatibility of growth and finite energy.

A singularity proof must also construct an actual divergence-free velocity and pressure, control the Navier–Stokes residual, and show the forcing has the required smoothness and decay through the singular time. The algebraic exponents do not supply those estimates. The research chapter reviews that construction separately and identifies exactly where the finite complex and order interpretation applies.
