\section{Wide cutoff corrections and full-frequency compensation}
\label{pca:wide-core}

Keep the pairing \eqref{pca:cross}, the exact ordered cutoff
identity \eqref{pca:ordered}, and all the original allocation,
primitive-conductor and raw-zero conditions. At one live
allocation put \(h=re_0\), \(E_1=Ne_1\), and write the
actual cutoff ideal as \(\zeta=e_0z_0=\zeta_1\zeta_2\).
Thus \(T_i=U/N\zeta_i\ge1\), \(E=Nd\), and
\[
 \Omega=\frac{E}{T_1T_2}=\frac{E N\zeta}{U^2}
                 <\frac{14}{E_1Nh}.
\]
Let \(\mathcal P\) consist of the good prime ideals of norms
seven or thirteen, with the original \(S\)-deletion. These
are at most four separate ideal labels. Put
\(s(E)=\mathbf1_{E\le T_1}+\mathbf1_{E\le T_2}-1\).
The exact wide correction is
\begin{equation}\label{pca:wide-factor}
 \begin{aligned}
 F_d(T_1,T_2)&=\mu(d)\bigl(s(E)+C_d(T_1,T_2)\bigr),\\
 C_d(T_1,T_2)&=
 \sum_{\substack{mn=d\\Nm>T_1,\;Nn>T_2}}
       \left(1-\sum_{\substack{p\in\mathcal P\\p\mid m}}
                  \mathbf1_{Nm>T_1Np}\right).
 \end{aligned}
\end{equation}
The two factors are coprime because \(d\) is squarefree.
The strict tests retain the original cutoff endpoints.
For \(E_1Nh\ge2\) only the unweighted factorization count
remains. For \(E_1Nh\ge14\) the correction vanishes.

At fixed row and fixed primal and dual labels, prescribe the
exact intersection \(\delta\) of \(d\) with the full forbidden
prime union, including \(\mathcal P\). In a divisor dyad
\(Y\le Nd<2Y\) put \(Y'=Y/N\delta\). For fixed \(\kappa>0\)
and \(Y'\ge D^\kappa\), the fixed-label scalar after taking
out its fixed coefficient and row phase has bound
\begin{equation}\label{pca:wide-scalar}
 \ll_\kappa \mathcal M_{24}(W)^2
 \frac{4^{\omega(\delta)}\log^C(2D)}{Y\,N\delta}\,
               \exp\!\bigl(-c_\kappa\sqrt{\log(2Y')}\bigr).
\end{equation}
The estimate includes both wide corrections, with the
original full prime deletions. It gives subpower cancellation
before a coherent average over the rows and other labels.

For each \(p\in\mathcal P\), replacing the partner coefficient
by its signed \(p\)-subtraction in \eqref{pca:wide-factor}
gives a zero primal amplitude \(V_d^{[p]}\).
The exact full-frequency complement is
\(V_d^{[p]}-\mathcal E_d^{[p]}=-\mathcal E_d^{[p]}\), where
\(\mathcal E_d^{[p]}\) is its middle equality projection.
The original elementary equality norm gives a contribution
of order at most \(R(DH)^\epsilon\mathcal M_{24}(W)^2\)
after the full divisor sum. This comparison requires all
partner modes, including the re-expressed direct component.

In the pure comparable sector \(A_1\asymp R\asymp D^{3/4}\),
\(A_2\asymp1\), \(B,K\asymp R\), the equality-side
wide correction reaches \(D^{3/4+\epsilon}\) on
\(Nd\le D^{13/56}\) with the inherited profile cost. Its
partner can carry the original coefficient, the step part,
or the correction part. Thus this prefix includes their
double correction. The remaining coherent wide sum and the
other signed components remain open.
